# Fixtures and game time

The public reader follows a signature-resolved competition manager to its owned fixture lists. It does not enumerate temporary copies found by scanning heap memory. On February 7, the current calendar-year collection contained 22 Wycombe fixtures: seven results and 15 scheduled games. All 22 rows were independently checked on the normal Senior Squad Schedule screen: opponent, venue, kickoff time, competition and available score. On February 17, all 22 rows matched again, including the three new results: Peterborough 4–2, Bolton 0–3 away, and Oxford 3–1. The same running API discarded the old club context and reconnected automatically with a new session ID.

The full types are `.?AVFIXTURE@sicomps@@` (read 0x58 bytes) and `.?AVFIXTURE_RESULT@sicomps@@` (read 0x80 bytes), complete-object offset zero. Their observed vtable RVAs are 0x5A25F58 and 0x5608078. Upcoming goals are null. Goal values do not determine penalty-shootout winners; extra-time, penalty and postponement flags have not been decoded. The public competition name omits stage/round suffixes. Team references deliberately say `club_name`, since a reserve team's full display name is not the club name.

## Coverage and consistency

`/fixtures` explicitly reports `calendar_year` and `as_of`. This is the loaded **calendar year**, not the whole football season. The July–December portion of the previous year is not included. Archived groups and their backing object storage remain research leads. The API rejects a loaded fixture year that differs from game time.

Every returned date must agree with its day bucket and calendar year. The reader rejects duplicate object references and unknown types. Owner lists, group pointers, returned records, manager identity, current club, year and game timestamp are checked again before returning. Read operations cannot provide an atomic snapshot while FM is processing a turn.

A research heap scan found 211 Wycombe fixture-shaped objects with many duplicates. Such a scan cannot establish ownership or freshness and is excluded from production. The team +0xA0 previous/next summaries are also lazily populated by the UI; null summaries after a reload do not prove there is no fixture. Neither shortcut is used to generate the public list.

## Packed time

Bits 0..8 store the ordinal day; high 16 bits store the year. Bits 9..15 are a quarter-hour slot: zero was observed at midnight; values 1..72 mean `06:00 + (slot-1)*15 minutes`. Slots above 72 remain unsupported. Game timestamps 00:00 and 08:00 were checked, and 40 schedule rows confirmed 12:45, 15:00, 19:45 and 20:00 encodings. Public times are FM's local display time, without an inferred real-world time zone.

This confirms a prior research lead from the locally inspected [Squad Analyzer notes](https://github.com/PhilipArmstead/Football-Manager-Squad-Analyzer/blob/master/NOTES.md); no external code was executed. Regressions use independently transcribed screenshots, not expectations generated by the decoder.
